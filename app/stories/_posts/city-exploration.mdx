Tokyo is a city that rewards those who wander. Every turn reveals something unexpected—a tiny shrine between skyscrapers, a jazz bar hidden underground.

## The Art of Getting Lost

I made a rule for this trip: no GPS for at least half the day. Some of my best discoveries came from wrong turns. A paper store run by a 90-year-old craftsman. A standing sushi bar with only four seats.

## Night Photography

Tokyo at night is a photographer's dream. The neon of Shinjuku, the quiet glow of residential streets, the last trains carrying tired workers home.

## What the City Taught Me

Efficiency and tradition can coexist beautifully. Every convenience store experience was a small lesson in customer service. Every temple visit a reminder to slow down.
