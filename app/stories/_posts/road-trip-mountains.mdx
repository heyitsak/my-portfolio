There's something magical about driving through mountains at dawn, when the fog lifts from the valleys and the peaks catch the first golden light.

## The Route

We started in Zurich, wound through the Gotthard Pass, and ended up in small villages that don't appear on most tourist maps. The plan was simple: follow the most interesting-looking roads.

## Hidden Gems

The best discovery was a tiny village called Soglio, perched on a mountainside with views that made us pull over every few minutes just to stare. The local bakery served the best bread I've ever tasted.

## Lessons Learned

Sometimes the best trips are the ones without a strict itinerary. We met locals, stumbled upon festivals, and found viewpoints that weren't in any guidebook. The mountains have a way of reminding you to slow down.
